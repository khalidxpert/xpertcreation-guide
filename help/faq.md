# Frequently asked questions

**Is it really free?**
Almost everything is free. A few optional items – boosts, the blue tick check, Company Pro and Store plans – are paid. See [Promote and paid items](../work/promote.md).

**The site looks old after an update.**
Close the app or tab and open it again. If it still looks old, clear the site's data in your browser settings.

**Why can't I message someone?**
Chat opens only after you are connected, or after a blood or donation request is accepted.

**Why is my profile not in XpertConnect?**
Add a headline, make sure your email is verified, and check that your profile is not set to "Only me".

**How do I get the blue tick?**
It is a paid identity check (Rs 999 once, refunded if rejected). See [The blue tick](../people/blue-tick.md). An ID check on its own gives you **ID verified**, which is free.

**How do I see what I paid for?**
Open [My orders](https://xpertcreation.com/orders) for active items, payments and receipts.

**Why can't I sell or organise meetups?**
You need an approved [ID check](../people/verify.md) first.

**Someone found my pet. How will I know?**
You get a notification as soon as they leave a note on your pet's tag page.
