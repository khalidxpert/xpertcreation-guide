# Frequently asked questions

**Is it really free?**
Yes. Everything on the site is free.

**The site looks old after an update.**
Close the app or tab and open it again. If it still looks old, clear the site's data in your browser settings.

**Why can't I message someone?**
Chat opens only after you are connected, or after a blood or donation request is accepted.

**Why is my profile not in People?**
Add a headline, make sure your email is verified, and check that your profile is not set to "Only me".

**How do I get the blue tick?**
Admins and moderators have it. Everyone else can apply at 1,000 followers.

**Someone found my pet. How will I know?**
You get a notification as soon as they leave a note on your pet's tag page.
