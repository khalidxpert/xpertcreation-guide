# Company pages

Every business can have a free company page on XpertConnect, at an address like `xpertcreation.com/company/your-company`.

## Creating your page
Tap your photo at the top, then **🏢 My company** (or choose **Company** when you sign up). Enter the company name, then fill in the details: tagline, about, industry, size, year founded, city, address, website, public email and phone, and upload a **logo** and **cover photo**. You can manage up to three companies.

## Step 1: verify your website
Enter your website and tap **Get my file**. Download `xpertcreation-verify.txt` and upload it to the main folder of your website, so it opens at `https://yourcompany.com/xpertcreation-verify.txt`. Tap **Check now**. When it passes, your page becomes **public** with a **🌐 website verified** mark.

## Step 2: verify your company (KYC)
For the **✔ Verified** badge, add:

- your **WhatsApp number** (our team will contact you on it), and
- proof such as the **registration certificate** or a **cheque copy** (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB). An NTN certificate helps too.

Tap **Send for review**. Our moderators check the documents and may contact you, then approve or explain what to fix. Documents are private: only you and our moderators can open them, and they are deleted 30 days after the decision. Once verified, the company name can only be changed through [support](../help/support.md).

## What a verified company can do
- **Post jobs**: only verified businesses can post on [Jobs](../community/jobs.md).
- People can **check in** at your company and **@mention** it; both link to your page.
- Your posts appear on your company page.
