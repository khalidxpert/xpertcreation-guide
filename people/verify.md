# Get ID verified

An ID check shows other members you are a real person. Approved members get the **✔ ID verified** mark.

## Why verify
You need it to:
- sell on [Freelance](../work/freelance.md) and the [Store](../work/store.md)
- start a group or organise [Meetups](../community/meetups.md)
- drive in [Ladies rides](../health/rides.md), or list property as an agent
- apply to be a [Brand Ambassador](../community/ambassadors.md)

## How
1. Open [xpertcreation.com/get-verified](https://xpertcreation.com/get-verified) (or tap your photo, then **Get verified**).
2. Add your **WhatsApp number**.
3. Upload your **CNIC (front and back)** or **passport**, and an **address proof** such as a utility bill or bank statement.
4. Tap **Send for review**.

Our team checks the documents and may contact you on WhatsApp. You get a notification when it is approved, or a note explaining what to fix.

## Your documents
Only you and our moderators can open them. Every opening is logged, and the files are deleted 30 days after the decision.

ID verified is free. It is not the blue tick – see [The blue tick](blue-tick.md).
