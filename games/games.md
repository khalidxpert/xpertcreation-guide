# Games and points

Thirteen games at [xpertcreation.com/games](https://xpertcreation.com/games), including chess, Ludo, sudoku, minesweeper, snake, 2048, Connect Four, Simon, a word game, tic-tac-toe and memory match.

## Points
Points come from two games only:
- **Memory match:** 1,000 × the best possible moves ÷ your moves, higher on medium and hard
- **Tic-tac-toe against the computer:** win 100, draw 40, lose 10; ×2 on medium and ×4 on hard

The other games run in your browser and send nothing, so they do not add points.
