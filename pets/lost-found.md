# Lost and found

At [xpertcreation.com/lost](https://xpertcreation.com/lost). Anyone can look; you need a verified account to post.

## Posting
Say whether the pet is lost or found, its kind, a short title, the city and where exactly, a few details and, if you want, a phone number. You can add a photo afterwards. If it is one of your own pets, choose it and it is marked as lost too.

## When it is sorted
Press **Resolved** to take the post down. Posts close by themselves after sixty days.

Adoption on XpertCreation is always free. We never allow pets to be sold here.
