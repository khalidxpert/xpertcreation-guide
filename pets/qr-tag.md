# The QR tag

Every pet gets a code and a QR tag. Print it from the pet's page and put it on the collar.

## When someone finds your pet
They scan the tag and see your pet's photo and name. If you added a phone number, they can call you or send a WhatsApp message.

They can also leave a note, even without an account. You get a notification at once, and the note is saved on your pet's page.

## Lost mode
Press **Mark as lost**. Anyone who scans the tag sees that your pet is lost. When it is home, press the button again.
