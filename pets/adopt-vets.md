# Adoption and vets

## Adopt a pet
At [xpertcreation.com/adopt](https://xpertcreation.com/adopt). Adoption here is always free: pets are given, never sold. Listings with a price are removed.

To give a pet a home, open a listing, write to the owner about your home, family and other pets, and tap **Send request**. If the owner accepts, a chat opens between you. **My requests** shows where each request stands.

To find a new home for a pet, tap **List a pet**: choose one of your pets or add details, pick the city, write about the pet and add up to four photos. You see everyone who asks; **Accept and chat** opens a chat, and **Mark as adopted** closes the listing.

Never pay for a pet or for "transport" in advance, and meet in a safe place.

## Vets
At [xpertcreation.com/vets](https://xpertcreation.com/vets). Find clinics by city, with phone numbers, hours, services and 24-hour emergency vets. Tap **Call** to ring them. Know a good vet? Add it at the bottom of the page; it appears once a moderator checks it.
