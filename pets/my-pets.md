# My pets

At [xpertcreation.com/pets](https://xpertcreation.com/pets).

## Adding a pet
Press **Add a pet** and fill in the name, kind (dog, cat, bird, rabbit, other), breed, gender, birthday and, if you want, a phone number to show on the tag. Add a photo afterwards (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB).

## Health records
Add vaccines, check-ups, deworming, medicine and weight, each with a date and, if needed, the next due date.

## The vaccine schedule
Press **Plan vaccines from the schedule**. Using your pet's birthday, the upcoming vaccines for its kind are added as *planned*. When one is given, press **Done**; if it repeats, the next one is planned for you.

## Reminders
Every morning at 9 we check what is due in the next three days and send you a notification. Each date is reminded once. The **Coming up** box shows what is due in the next month, and anything overdue.
