# Vocabulary and Learn Norwegian

## English vocabulary
At [xpertcreation.com/vocabulary](https://xpertcreation.com/vocabulary) (also in the menu). English words with their **Urdu meaning**, an example sentence and similar words, in four levels: **Beginner**, **Everyday**, **IELTS / Academic** and **Business English**.

- **Today**: the word of the day (also on the home page).
- **Learn**: flashcards. Tap **Show meaning**, then **Knew it** or **Didn't know**. Words you know come back later; words you miss come back sooner.
- **Quiz**: ten questions, English to meaning or Urdu to English.
- **Words**: the full list, with search.

Tap **🔊** to hear a word. Practise every day to keep your **🔥 streak**. Signed in, your progress is saved to your account on every device.

## Learn Norwegian
At [xpertcreation.com/norwegian](https://xpertcreation.com/norwegian). Norwegian words and phrases with their **English and Urdu meanings**, a Norwegian example sentence with its translation, and Norwegian pronunciation, in three levels: **Basics**, **Everyday life** and **Work and life in Norway** (jobs, tax card, NAV, doctor and more). It works the same way as Vocabulary.

If 🔊 does not sound Norwegian, install a Norwegian voice on your phone (Android: Settings, Text-to-speech, Google, Norwegian).

The whole site can also be shown in Norwegian: choose **Norsk** in the language menu on the home page.
