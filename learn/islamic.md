# Islamic corner

At [xpertcreation.com/islamic](https://xpertcreation.com/islamic).

- **Ayah of the day**: one verse each day in Arabic, with Urdu (Fateh Muhammad Jalandhari) and English (Saheeh International) translations, and a link to read the surah with tafsir on quran.com. The text and translations come from AlQuran Cloud (Tanzil).
- **Hijri date**, and a **Ramadan countdown**. Dates may differ by a day from the local moon sighting by the Ruet-e-Hilal Committee.
- On Fridays, **Jumma Mubarak**.
- Quick links to prayer times, Qibla direction, the zakat calculator and hadith collections on sunnah.com.
