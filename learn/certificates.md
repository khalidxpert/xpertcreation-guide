# Certificates and badges

## Getting a certificate
1. Finish the course and pass its quiz.
2. Leave a short review of the course.
3. Your certificate opens. Share it, or ask your friends to join.

## Checking a certificate
Anyone can check that a certificate is real at [xpertcreation.com/verify](https://xpertcreation.com/verify).

## Badges and roles
As you learn you earn roles (Learner, Scholar, Content Creator, Mentor) and medals in seven areas, each with four tiers. Your role appears on the Academy home page.
