# Star signs and the real sky

At [xpertcreation.com/zodiac](https://xpertcreation.com/zodiac).

## The real sky
Today's Moon phase, how much of it is lit, the next full and new moon, and which sign the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn are in. These positions are real astronomy, worked out on your device.

## Your Sun, Moon and Rising sign
Enter your date of birth, the time if you know it, and where you were born. Without a time you still get your Sun sign. The Rising sign needs the time. If your birthday is on your account, it fills in by itself.

Star signs are tradition and entertainment, not science. Please do not use them to make real decisions.

For the birth chart, pick your country, state and city from the lists: any city in the world works, and its map position and time zone are filled in for you. Check the time zone if you were born during summer time.
