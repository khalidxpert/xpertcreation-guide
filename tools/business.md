# Business: staff, stock and attendance

At [xpertcreation.com/business](https://xpertcreation.com/business). Free, for a shop, office or small team.

**Your data stays on your device.** Nothing is sent to our server, so only you can see it. It also means that clearing the browser or losing the phone loses it, so download a backup every week.

## Employees
Add each person's name, job, phone, monthly salary and joining date. When someone leaves, press **Left**: their records stay for your reports.

## Stock
Add items with a code, category, unit, cost and sale price, and the level at which you want a warning. Use **+ In** when stock arrives and **− Out** when it is sold or used, with a note if you like. Items running low are shown in red, and the top shows the total value of your stock.

## Attendance
Pick a date and mark each person **P** (present), **H** (half day), **L** (leave) or **A** (absent). Add the time in and out to count hours. **Mark everyone present** saves time on normal days. Everything saves by itself.

## Monthly report
Days present, on leave and absent, hours, overtime beyond your normal working day, and an estimate of pay. Download it for Excel or print it.

## Backup and settings
Download a backup and restore it on another phone or computer. Set your business name, currency and normal working hours.
