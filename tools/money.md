# Money

At [xpertcreation.com/money](https://xpertcreation.com/money). Keep track of cash, JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank balances in one place. It is saved to your XpertCreation account, so you see it on all your devices.

- **+ Received** – money coming in, to any of your accounts.
- **− Paid** – money going out, with what it was for.
- **↔ Move** – from one account to another, for example bank to JazzCash.

Each account shows its balance, and the list shows every entry.

Money only keeps your own records. It does not move real money or connect to your bank.
