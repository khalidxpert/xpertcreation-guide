# Business cards

## Your digital card
At [xpertcreation.com/cards](https://xpertcreation.com/cards). Make a free digital visiting card with its own link, like `xpertcreation.com/c/your-name`, and a QR code.

The editor has these steps:

- **1 · Content**: photo, logo, name, title, company, a short introduction, quick buttons (call, WhatsApp, email, website, location), and sections such as About, Contact, Follow me, Web links, Gallery, Business hours, Video and **Schedule a meeting** (paste a Google Calendar or Calendly booking link to show a **Book an appointment** button).
- **2 · Design**: 13 looks, each shown as a picture, including Mandala, Black & gold, Green geometric, Clinic, Shop, Tech dark and Maroon. Pick one and the preview changes.
- **3 · QR code**: style and download your QR code.
- **✔ Verify**: upload a small file to your website to show **Verified: yourcompany.com** on your card.
- **Stats**: how many people opened your card and tapped each button.
- **🖨️ Print**: printable cards (below).

Press **Save**. On your own card, a blue **✏️ Edit card** button appears at the top when you are signed in. Visitors can tap **Add to contacts** to save your details. Your card also appears as **📇 Digital card** on your XpertConnect profile.

## Printable cards
At [xpertcreation.com/cards/print](https://xpertcreation.com/cards/print), or **🖨️ Print** in the editor. Pick one of 10 designs, fill in your details and add a logo if you like. The back has a QR code to your digital card.

Download **front + back (PNG)**, the **card as a PDF**, or **10 cards on an A4 sheet**. Print on thick card paper (300 to 350 gsm) at **100% / actual size**, not "fit to page". On the A4 sheet, page 2 has the backs lined up for double-sided printing. Everything is made on your phone or computer; nothing is uploaded.
