# Gold, dollar and petrol rates

At [xpertcreation.com/rates](https://xpertcreation.com/rates), and the **💰 Today's rates** card on the home page.

## What you see
- **Gold** (24 and 22 karat, per tola), **silver**, **petrol**, **high-speed diesel** and **LPG**, with the change since yesterday and a 30-day chart.
- **Currencies** in rupees for one unit: US dollar, euro, pound, Saudi riyal, UAE dirham, Canadian and Australian dollar, Chinese yuan, Kuwaiti dinar, Qatari riyal, Malaysian ringgit and Norwegian krone.
- A **converter**: type an amount, pick a currency, and see it in rupees.

Tap **Share on WhatsApp** to send today's rates to anyone.

## Where the rates come from
Currency rates refresh automatically every few hours from a public exchange-rate service; they are mid-market rates, so banks and the open market may differ slightly. Gold, silver and fuel prices are updated by the XpertCreation team from official announcements and the market. All rates are a guide only: check with your bank or dealer before buying or selling.
