# Currency rates

At [xpertcreation.com/rates](https://xpertcreation.com/rates), and the **💰 Today's rates** card on the home page.

Rupee rates for one unit of 12 currencies: US dollar, euro, pound, Saudi riyal, UAE dirham, Canadian and Australian dollar, Chinese yuan, Kuwaiti dinar, Qatari riyal, Malaysian ringgit and Norwegian krone, with the change since yesterday and a 30-day chart. Use the **converter** to turn any amount into rupees, and **Share on WhatsApp** to send today's rates.

Rates refresh automatically every few hours from a public exchange-rate service. They are mid-market rates, so banks and the open market may differ slightly; check with your bank or money changer before you buy or sell.
