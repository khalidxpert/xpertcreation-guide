# Travel in Pakistan

At [xpertcreation.com/travel](https://xpertcreation.com/travel).

- **Emergency numbers** you can tap to call: 1122 (rescue), 15 (police), 130 (Motorway Police), 16 (fire), 115 (Edhi) and 1020 (Chhipa).
- **Trip tools**: trip cost, fuel cost, weather and currency rates.
- Links to the official sites of **Pakistan Railways** (timings and tickets), **NHA** (tolls and M-Tag) and the **Motorway Police** (road conditions and fog).
