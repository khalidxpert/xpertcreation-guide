# The Xpert app

XpertCreation has an Android app called **Xpert**. It opens the whole site in a full-screen app, with notifications and a home-screen icon.

## Getting it
1. Open Google Play and search **Xpert XpertCreation** (package com.xpertcreation.app).
2. Tap **Install**, then open it and sign in with your XpertCreation account.

Your account, posts, courses and orders are the same in the app and on the website.

## Shortcuts
Press and hold the app icon to jump straight to **Courses**.

## Paying in the app
In the app, paid items such as boosts are paid with **Google Play**. On the website you pay by card or JazzCash. See [Promote and paid items](../work/promote.md).

## Updates
The app updates from Google Play. If something looks old, close the app and open it again.

On an iPhone, open xpertcreation.com in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
