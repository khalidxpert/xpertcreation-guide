# Privacy and safety

## What other people can see
- **Country counts** are public: how many members are in each country.
- **Newest members** appear in the footer with a first name and an initial, for example "Khalid S.".
- **Moderators** can see which members are online.
- Your **professional profile** is visible to signed-in members by default. You can make it public (and findable on Google) or hide it completely.

You can hide your name from the public lists in your privacy settings.

## What nobody else sees
Your email address and your password are never shown to anyone. Your pets' health records are yours alone.

## Chat is not open to strangers
Nobody can start a chat with you out of the blue. A chat opens only after you have agreed to something: accepting a connection request, a blood request or a donation.

## Reporting
Profiles, reviews and posts can be reported. Moderators look at every report.

## Security log
To keep the site safe we keep a security log of actions on your account - for example signing in, posting, or sending a message - with the time, IP address and device. What you write is never copied into it. Only the site owner can see it, and each line is deleted after 180 days.

## Interests and privacy settings
Tap your photo at the top, then **Interests & privacy** (or go to [xpertcreation.com/me/preferences](https://xpertcreation.com/me/preferences)).

- **Your interests**: pick up to 12. They show as chips at the top of XpertConnect, each opening posts with that hashtag.
- **Gender (optional)**: never shown on your profile. It is only used for the "same gender" choices and for women-only or men-only groups.
- **Who can find me in search**: Everyone, Same gender only, or My connections only. This covers people search and @ suggestions.
- **Who can send me connection requests**: Everyone, Same gender only, or Nobody. Private messages always need a connection first.

When you create a group you can make it **women only** or **men only**; only members with that gender (set in these settings) can join or be added.
