# Pin your favourite sections

Keep the tools and sections you use most one tap away – for example Money, Freelance or Property.

## Pinning
- On any page, tap the **📌 pin** button at the top, next to the menu. It turns gold when the page is pinned.
- Or tap **+ Pin a tool or section** in your pinned cards, search or scroll the list (sorted A–Z) and tap one.

Pinned sections show as gold cards for quick access, and are highlighted in the menu.

## Limits and removing
You can pin up to **3**. To pin another, first remove one: tap **×** on its card, or tap the gold pin button again on that page.
