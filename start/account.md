# Your account

## Creating an account
1. Open [xpertcreation.com/register](https://xpertcreation.com/register).
2. Enter your name, email address and a password.
3. We send a code to your email. Enter it to verify your address.

Some things need a verified email: posting on the lost & found board, endorsing people, sending connection requests and writing reviews. This keeps fake accounts out.

## Your account page
At [xpertcreation.com/account](https://xpertcreation.com/account) you can:
- change your name and password
- choose an avatar or upload a photo
- add your birthday (it fills in the age calculator and your star sign by itself)
- add a WhatsApp number
- change your privacy settings
- open **Social links** to add your Facebook, Instagram, X and other accounts to your profile

## Deleting your account
Go to [xpertcreation.com/account/delete](https://xpertcreation.com/account/delete). Deleting removes your account and what belongs to it. It cannot be undone.
