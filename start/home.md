# The home page

When you are signed in, the home page greets you by name with your country's flag, the time and date, and a few quick links.

## Profile completion
If your professional profile is not finished, a box shows how complete it is and what is missing: a photo, a headline, your city, a few lines about you, three skills, a job and your education. Each item takes you to the right place. Press **Later** to hide it for three days.

## The tiles
Each tile opens a main part of the site – for example XpertConnect, Freelance, the Store, Academy, Tools, Money, Pets, Shows and Chat. The tiles follow the language you choose. Your [pinned sections](pins.md) show as gold cards.

## The top bar
Academy, Tools, Games and **Meetups** are in the top menu; the round icons open Home, Meetups, Chat and Settings.

## Where our members are
Near the bottom you can see how many members there are, in which countries, and how many are online right now.

## The footer
Every page ends with the same footer: our address, WhatsApp, email, links to the main sections, and a live strip of members who joined recently.

## Account and profile
Your account and your professional profile are one place with two tabs at the top: **Account** and **Professional profile**.
