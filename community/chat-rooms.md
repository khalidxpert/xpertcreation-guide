# Chat rooms (IRC)

At [xpertcreation.com/chat-rooms](https://xpertcreation.com/chat-rooms). Free live chat rooms on XpertCreation's own IRC network. Guests are welcome.

## Rooms
- **#xpertcreation** – the main hall: say hello and ask anything
- **#xpertconnect** – networking and opportunities
- **#xpertacademy** – study help and courses
- **#shairi** – Urdu poetry: type **!shair** for a couplet
- **#jobs** – job posts, CVs and interviews
- **#pakistan**, **#tech**, **#games** and more

Anyone can register a new room.

## Joining
Tap **Open chat rooms** to chat in your browser. Sign in with your XpertCreation account to keep your nickname.

Prefer a classic IRC app such as **mIRC**? Connect to **irc.xpertcreation.com**.

Be kind. Moderators can warn, mute or ban people who break the rules. For private one-to-one messages, use [Chat](chat.md).
