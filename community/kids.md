# Kids

At [xpertcreation.com/kids](https://xpertcreation.com/kids). Cartoons, rhymes, stories, Islamic and learning videos for children, from **official YouTube channels chosen by the XpertCreation team**.

- Pick a type (Islamic, Learning, Rhymes, Stories, Cartoons) and an age (2–5 or 6–10).
- Tap a video to play it full screen; tap **Close** to come back.
- There are no comments or chat on this page, no sign-in is needed, and we do not collect any information from children.
- Videos play in YouTube's privacy-enhanced player and only suggest videos from the same channel.

Parents: please stay nearby when young children watch. If you see a video that should not be there, tell us at [xpertcreation.com/support](https://xpertcreation.com/support).
