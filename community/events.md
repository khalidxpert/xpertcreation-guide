# Events

At [xpertcreation.com/events](https://xpertcreation.com/events). Meetups, classes, webinars and get-togethers that anyone can create.

- **Upcoming**, **My events** and **Past** tabs.
- Open an event and tap **✅ Going** or **⭐ Interested**. We remind you **a day before and an hour before**.
- Online events show the joining link after you tap Going or Interested. In-person events have a **Map** link.
- **↗ Share** sends the event to friends.

## Creating an event
Tap **+ Create event**: name, start (and end) time, in person or online, address or meeting link, city, details and a cover picture. If you cancel it, everyone who tapped Going or Interested gets a notification.

For regular groups that meet again and again, use [Meetups](meetups.md).
