# Feed

At [xpertcreation.com/feed](https://xpertcreation.com/feed). Share news, thoughts and photos with other members.

## Posting
Write in the box at the top, add up to four photos, and choose who can see it: **Everyone**, **Members** (signed in) or **Connections** only. You can edit or delete your own posts from the three dots.

## Reacting and commenting
Tap **Like** to pick a reaction: Like, Celebrate, Support, Love, Insightful or Funny. Tap it again to take it back. Tap **Comment** to join in, and **Reply** to answer someone. You get a notification when people comment on your post or reply to you.

## Tabs
**For you** shows everything you can see. **Following** shows people you follow and your connections. **My posts** shows your own posts.

## Sharing and safety
**Share** opens your phone's share options, or copies the link. Every post has its own link. Use **Report** on a post or comment that breaks the rules; moderators can hide it.
