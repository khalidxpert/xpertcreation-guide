# XpertConnect

At [xpertcreation.com/feed](https://xpertcreation.com/feed). XpertConnect is where members share news, thoughts and photos, follow each other and grow their network. It is second in the menu, in blue.

## Creating a post
Tap **What's on your mind?** at the top. The **Create post** screen opens:

- Choose who can see it under your name: **Everyone**, **Members** (signed in) or **Connections**.
- **🖼️ Photo**: add up to four photos.
- **👥 Tag people**: add up to ten people who are with you. They get a notification, if they can see the post.
- **😊 Feeling**: happy, blessed, celebrating, travelling, working and more.
- **📍 Check-in**: where you are. If the place is a company on XpertConnect, your post links to its company page; otherwise it opens in Google Maps.
- **🎨 Background**: for short text posts (up to 300 characters, no photo), pick one of 12 colours and a text colour. Your text shows big and centred, like a poster.

Your post then reads like: *Khalid is 😊 happy at 📍 Lahore with Ali and Sara.*

## Mentions and hashtags
Type **@** and a few letters to mention someone; pick them from the list. They get a notification if they can see the post. Companies can be mentioned too (shown with 🏢); the company's owner is told.

Write **#something** to add a hashtag. Tapping a hashtag opens a page with every public post that uses it. If a hashtag matches a company's name (like #xpertcreation), the company is shown at the top of that page.

## Search
Tap **🔍 Search XpertConnect** next to the heading to find people, companies and hashtags.

## Reacting and commenting
Tap **Like** on a post to pick a reaction. Tap the reactions count (for example 👍❤️ 12) to see **who reacted**, with a **Connect** button for people you are not connected with yet.

Under each comment you can **Reply** or **Like** it (👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 👏). The comment's writer gets a notification.

## Your wall
Tap your photo at the top, then **🧱 My wall**, to see all your own posts. You can post from your own wall too. Tapping anyone's name or photo opens their profile, or their posts if they have no profile yet.

## Tabs
**For you** shows everything you can see. **Following** shows people you follow and your connections. **My posts** shows your own posts.

## Sharing and safety
**Share** opens your phone's share options, or copies the link. Every post has its own link. Use **Report** on a post or comment that breaks the rules; moderators can hide it.
