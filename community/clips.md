# XpertClips

At [xpertcreation.com/clips](https://xpertcreation.com/clips). Short vertical videos, like TikTok or Reels.

**Watching:** swipe up for the next clip. Tap to pause, **double-tap** to like. On the right: the creator (tap to see all their clips), ❤️ like, 💬 comments, ↗ share, and 🔇 sound on or off.

**Posting:** tap **+ Post**, choose a video (up to **60 seconds**), add a caption with #hashtags and tap **Post**. We prepare your video so it plays on every phone; it usually takes about a minute and you get a notification when it is live. Your first clips are checked by our team; after that they go live straight away. You can post up to 5 clips a day.

**My clips** shows everything you posted, with its status, and lets you delete a clip.

**Rules:** keep clips family-friendly, and only post videos you made or have the right to share. Use **⋯ → Report** on anything that breaks the rules; clips with several reports are hidden until our team checks them.
