# Jobs

At [xpertcreation.com/jobs](https://xpertcreation.com/jobs). Posting, applying and hiring are all free.

## Finding a job
Search by title, company or city, and filter by type (full-time, part-time, internship, freelance, contract) and on-site, remote or hybrid. **For you** shows open jobs that ask for skills on your profile.

## Applying
Open a job and tap **Apply**. The employer sees your Connect profile - headline, skills and experience - and a short note if you write one, so keep your profile up to date. **My applications** shows where each one stands: applied, shortlisted or not selected. You can withdraw an application while it is still waiting.

## Posting a job
Tap **Post a job**: title, company, type, location, an optional salary, the skills you need, a description and the last date to apply (up to 90 days). You can edit, close or delete it later.

## Hiring
On your job's page you see everyone who applied. **Shortlist and chat** tells the person and opens a chat between you. **Not selected** lets them know politely.

## Stay safe
Jobs here are free. Never pay a fee, deposit or "registration charge" to get a job. Report any job that asks for money; moderators remove it.
