# Library

At [xpertcreation.com/library](https://xpertcreation.com/library). Free books you can read on XpertCreation: Urdu classics, Islamic books, English classics, poetry, children's and learning books.

- Search by title or author, or pick a category and a language.
- Tap a book, then **Read here** (Internet Archive books open in a page-turning reader inside the site) or **Read on** the source website.
- Tap **🔖 Save** to keep a book in your list. Books you opened recently show under **Continue reading**.

All books come from trusted free sources (Internet Archive, Project Gutenberg and Wikisource) and are public domain or openly licensed. The XpertCreation team chooses which books are added.
