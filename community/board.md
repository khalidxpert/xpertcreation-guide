# Jobs, results, admissions and scholarships

At [xpertcreation.com/board](https://xpertcreation.com/board). A list of **government jobs**, **exam results**, **university admissions** and **scholarships** in Pakistan, added by the XpertCreation team.

Each one shows the organisation, city and last date, with a **days left** badge (red when 3 days or fewer remain), and an **Open official link** button. Always read the full notice and apply on the official website; XpertCreation never asks for fees to apply.

Notices disappear automatically after their last date. Use the tabs to see only jobs, results, admissions or scholarships.

For private-sector jobs from verified companies, see [Jobs](jobs.md).
