# Predict the winner

At [xpertcreation.com/predict](https://xpertcreation.com/predict). Pick who will win upcoming cricket matches before they start. Each right pick earns **10 points**. You can change your pick until the match begins.

You can see how other members picked (as percentages). After the match, results are added by the XpertCreation team and points go to everyone who got it right. See **🏆 Leaderboard** for this month and all time.

It is just for fun: there is no money, betting or prizes involved.
