# Birthdays, news and weather

- **Birthdays:** reminders for the birthdays you care about, every year.
- **News:** the latest headlines.
- **Weather:** today and the days ahead, where you are.
- **Money:** cash, wallets and bank balances in one place, kept on your phone only.
- **Visiting card:** your own digital card with a QR code, ready to share.
- **Vibe check:** today's mood, from one question.
