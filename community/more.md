# Birthdays, news and weather

- **Birthdays:** reminders for the birthdays you care about, every year.
- **News:** the latest headlines.
- **Weather:** today and the days ahead, where you are.
- **Money:** cash, wallets and bank balances in one place, kept on your phone only.
- **Visiting card:** your own digital card with a QR code, ready to share.
- **Vibe check:** today's mood, from one question.

## Visiting card QR
In the QR code tool, tap **Visiting card** and fill in your name, designation, company, phone, WhatsApp, email, website and address; tap **+ Add field** for anything else. Scanning it offers "Add to contacts" on any phone. Download it as PNG or SVG (best for printing), copy it, or share it. The QR is made on your device - nothing you type is sent anywhere.
