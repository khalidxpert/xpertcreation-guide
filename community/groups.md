# Groups and channels

Groups live in [Chat](https://xpertcreation.com/chat). There are two kinds:

- **👥 Group**: everyone in it can talk.
- **📢 Channel**: only admins post; members read and react. Good for announcements.

## Creating one
In Chat, tap **+ New group**. Choose **Group** or **Channel**, give it a name, a photo and a **purpose**, and add people from your connections. People who only let their connections add them (or nobody) get an invitation instead. Tick **Show in the groups directory** if anyone should be able to find and join it.

## Inviting people
Admins can share the group's **invite link**. Anyone who opens it can join (unless the group is full or they were removed).

## Roles
👑 **Owner** (who made it), 🛡️ **Admin**, ⭐ **Moderator**, and 🤖 the **group bot**. Admins can change the name, photo, who can send messages and the invite link.

## The groups directory
At [xpertcreation.com/groups](https://xpertcreation.com/groups) (also **🔎 Find groups** in Chat). It lists the groups and channels whose admins chose to show them, with name, purpose, creator and date. Rankings:

- 🏆 **Most members**
- 🔥 **Most active this week**
- 📈 **Fastest growing**
- ✨ **New**
- 👑 **Top creators** (people whose groups have the most members)

Tap **Join** to go in. If you are an admin, **Your groups** at the bottom lets you show or hide each of your groups in the directory. Private groups never appear.

## The XpertCreation channel
Every member is added to the official **XpertCreation** channel, with daily news, tools, sports and drama posts.
