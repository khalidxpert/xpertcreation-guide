# Roots of Love – family tree

At [xpertcreation.com/roots](https://xpertcreation.com/roots). Build your family tree (shajra) together with your whole family.

- **🌳 Family tree** – add grandparents, parents, uncles, aunts, cousins and children.
- **🤝 Relation finder** – see how two people are related: Chacha, Phuphi, Mamu, Khala and more.
- **📞 Family phone book**, **🖼️ Album** and **📌 Notice board** for the family.
- **🎂 Birthday and barsi reminders**.

Your tree is **private to the family** – only the members you invite can see it.
