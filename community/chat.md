# Chat

At [xpertcreation.com/chat](https://xpertcreation.com/chat). Chat opens only between people who have agreed to something: an accepted connection, a blood request or a donation. Nobody can message you out of the blue.

## In a chat
- New messages arrive by themselves while the chat is open.
- One tick means sent; two blue ticks mean the other person has read it.
- You can see when the other person is typing.
- Tap the paper clip to send a photo (up to 2 MB, up to 20 a day).

## Unread messages
A red number next to **Chat** in the menu shows unread messages. The home page shows your three most recent chats.

## Block and report
Tap the three dots at the top of a chat. **Block** stops both of you from sending messages there; you can unblock later. **Report** sends the chat to our moderators.
