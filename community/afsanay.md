# Afsanay

At [xpertcreation.com/afsanay](https://xpertcreation.com/afsanay). Stories written by XpertCreation members, in Urdu or English, published qist by qist (episode by episode).

**Reading:** pick a category (Family, Romance, Social, Islamic, Mystery, Humour, Real life, For children), tap a story and **Start reading**. Use **🔖 Save** to keep it in your list, **❤️ Like** it, and **+ Follow writer** to get a notification when they publish the next qist.

**Writing:** tap **✍️ Write a story**, add a title, category and language, write the first qist (at least 200 characters) and publish. Later, open your story and use **Add the next qist**. Tick "This is the last qist" when the story is complete.

**Rules:** only publish **your own writing**. Copying from digests, books or websites is not allowed, and keep stories family-friendly. Readers can report a story; copied or harmful stories are removed.
