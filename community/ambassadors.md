# Brand Ambassadors

At [xpertcreation.com/ambassadors](https://xpertcreation.com/ambassadors). Brand Ambassadors represent XpertCreation in their city and community and help people get started with XpertConnect, Freelance, the Store and everything else.

Every ambassador has the **blue tick** and a gold star badge on their profile and posts.

## Applying
1. Complete your [XpertConnect profile](../people/profile.md).
2. Get your [ID verified](../people/verify.md).
3. Open the Ambassadors page and tap **Apply**, telling us about your city and how you will help.

Our team reviews every application. Nobody is added automatically.
