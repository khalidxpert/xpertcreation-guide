# Recipes

At [xpertcreation.com/recipes](https://xpertcreation.com/recipes). Pakistani recipes from the XpertCreation kitchen and from members: karahi, biryani, daal chawal, pulao, kebabs, snacks, sweets and more.

Open a recipe and use **Serves − / +** to change the number of people; all amounts change with it.

## Share your recipe
Tap **+ Share your recipe**, add the name, the ingredients (one per line, like `1 | kg | chicken`), the steps and a photo if you like. It appears after a quick check by our team.
