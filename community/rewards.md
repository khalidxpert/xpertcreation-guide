# Rewards and invites

At [xpertcreation.com/rewards](https://xpertcreation.com/rewards). Free for every member, with every prize listed on the page.

## Points and the lucky wheel
While a rewards campaign is running:
- Use XpertCreation – read, post, learn, use tools – to earn points.
- Enough points in a day gives you a **daily spin** of the lucky wheel; using several different sections can give a **bonus spin**.
- You need a verified email. Some prizes also ask you to post about the rewards on XpertConnect, say hello in IRC **#xpertcreation**, and follow our YouTube and Facebook.

Winnings go to your rewards balance. Withdraw to **EasyPaisa**, **JazzCash** or a bank account, take **mobile load**, or spend it on the site. A transparent leaderboard shows points and spins.

## Invite friends
Your personal invite link is **xpertcreation.com/r/your-username**. Share it on WhatsApp, Facebook or anywhere. Friends who join with it are counted as your invites, and when they pay for something on XpertCreation you get a share added to your balance. Invite contests and draws are listed on the Rewards page.

The exact prizes and rules for the current campaign are always on the Rewards page.
