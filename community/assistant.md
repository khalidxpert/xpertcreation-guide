# AI Assistant

At [xpertcreation.com/assistant](https://xpertcreation.com/assistant). A free AI helper for signed-in members. Ask about your studies, Excel or Python, writing a CV or a post, ideas, or how to use the site - in Urdu, English or Roman Urdu.

- Each member can ask a set number of questions a day; the page shows how many are left.
- Your chat is kept only on your own phone or computer. **New chat** clears it.
- The assistant can make mistakes. Check important facts, and for health, legal or money matters speak to a qualified person.
- Never type passwords, CNIC numbers or card numbers into it.
