# Meetups

At [xpertcreation.com/meetups](https://xpertcreation.com/meetups) (also in the menu and on the home page). Groups for people in your city who share an interest – tech, business, careers, sports, arts, food, languages, faith and more.

## Joining
1. Open **Meetups** or **Groups** and search by city or tap a topic.
2. Open a group and tap **+ Join group**. You get a notification whenever it schedules a meetup.
3. Open a meetup and tap **I'm going**. You get a reminder a day before.

Each meetup shows the date and time, how long it lasts, the place with **Directions** (and a map when the organiser pinned it), who is going and how many seats are left. For online meetups, the joining link appears once you tap Going.

When the seats are full you join the **waitlist**. If someone cancels, the first person waiting gets the seat automatically and a notification. Tap **Can't go** to free your seat.

**Mine** shows your groups and the meetups you are going to. After a meetup, people who went can leave a **review**.

## Organising
Only members with an approved [ID check](../people/verify.md) can start groups and organise meetups, because people meet organisers in person.

1. Tap **+ Start a group**: name, topic, city, a few lines about it and a cover picture.
2. In your group, tap **📅 Schedule a meetup**: title, date and time, length, in person (venue, city, optional map pin from your location) or online (meeting link), seats (0 = no limit) and details.
3. All members are notified. You can edit or delete the group and cancel a meetup – everyone going is told.

See also [Events](events.md) for one-off events.
