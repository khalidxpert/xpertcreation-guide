# Status

Status lets you share a photo or a short message that disappears after **24 hours**. Find it at the top of [XpertConnect](https://xpertcreation.com/feed), in the row of round photos.

## Sharing a status
Tap **+ Your story**, then:

- **📷 Photo**: pick a photo, add a caption if you like.
- **✍️ Text**: type a message and pick a background colour.

Choose who can see it: **My connections** or **Everyone**, then tap **Share to your story**. You can share up to 15 a day.

## Watching
People with something new have a **coloured ring** around their photo; it turns grey once you have seen everything. Tap a photo to watch. Each one shows for 5 seconds; tap the **right side** for the next, the **left side** to go back, or ✕ to close.

## Your own status
Open your own circle to see **👁 Seen by** (who watched it) or **🗑 Delete** it. After 24 hours it is removed automatically, photo included.
