# Step counter

At [xpertcreation.com/steps](https://xpertcreation.com/steps). It counts your steps with your phone's motion sensor and draws your walk, run or ride live on a map, with distance, time, pace and calories. Signed in, your walks are saved to your account.

## A walk, run or ride
1. Choose **🚶 Walk**, **🏃 Run** or **🚲 Ride**.
2. Tap **▶ Start**. Allow **location** (for the map) and **motion** (for the steps) when the phone asks.
3. The map turns with you, with an arrow showing the way you face. Tap the compass for north-up, or the target to follow yourself again.
4. Tap **❚❚ Pause** to take a break and **■ Finish** at the end.

Every new walk starts from zero. Today's total keeps adding up.

**Keep the page open while you move.** The screen stays on during a walk. Phone browsers stop GPS and the motion sensor when the screen is off or you switch apps, so counting pauses until you come back.

## Goal, streak and history
- **🎯 Set goal**: your daily steps (8,000 to start). The ring fills as you walk.
- **🔥 Streak**: reach your goal day after day to grow it.
- **Today, hour by hour**: a chart of when you were active.
- **This week**, **My walks** and **Daily totals**: every walk and every day.
- **Settings**: daily goal, height (sets your step length when the phone has no motion sensor) and weight (for calories). Calories are an estimate.

## Sharing
When you reach your goal, share it with **Post on XpertConnect**, WhatsApp, Facebook or X, and invite friends to walk with you.
