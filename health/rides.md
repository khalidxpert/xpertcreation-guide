# Ladies rides

At [xpertcreation.com/rides](https://xpertcreation.com/rides). Safe rides by women, for women: car, rickshaw or bike, in every city.

## Booking a ride
1. Open **🚗 Ride** and set your pickup and drop-off on the map. You see the road route and the **fare before you book**.
2. Choose a car, rickshaw or bike and request the ride.
3. A nearby woman driver accepts. Follow her on the live map.
4. Pay the driver **in cash** at the end.

## Safety
- Every driver is a woman whose **ID, licence and vehicle papers** were checked by our team.
- **Share trip** sends your live journey to family.
- The **🚨 SOS** button is always on screen during a ride.

**My rides** lists your trips.

## Driving
Open **👩‍✈️ Drive** to apply. You need an approved [ID check](../people/verify.md) plus your licence and vehicle papers. Our team reviews them before you can take rides.
