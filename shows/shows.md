# Dramas and films

At [xpertcreation.com/shows](https://xpertcreation.com/shows).

## Browsing
Tabs for Pakistani dramas, Indian dramas, Pakistani films, Indian films and what is trending this week, or search by name.

## A drama or film page
The story, cast, genres, seasons and episodes, the trailer, where to watch it in Pakistan, and ratings from TMDB and from our members. Where a channel posts the episodes itself, there is a button to watch them on its official YouTube channel.

## Your list and your reviews
Press **Add to my list** to keep a list of what you want to watch. Rate from one to five stars and write a few words if you like. One review per person per title.

We only link to official channels, never to copies.

Film and drama information and images come from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
