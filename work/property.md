# Property

Inside Freelance at [xpertcreation.com/market](https://xpertcreation.com/market) – tap **🏠 Property**. Houses, flats, plots and shops for sale or rent.

## Finding a place
Search by city, sale or rent, type and price. Open a listing for photos, details and the map, then contact the owner or agent directly by call or WhatsApp. No payment goes through XpertCreation.

## Listing your property
Any member with a verified email can list their own property: add the title, sale or rent, price, city and area, size, photos and how to contact you.

**Agents and dealers** need an approved [ID check](../people/verify.md) first.

A **Property boost** puts your listing at the top of Property search in its city, with a gold Featured badge. See [Promote and paid items](promote.md).

Always visit a property and check the documents before you pay anyone.
