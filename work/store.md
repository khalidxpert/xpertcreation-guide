# Store

At [xpertcreation.com/store](https://xpertcreation.com/store). Phones, fashion, home, grocery and more from verified local sellers.

## Shopping
1. Browse **Home** and **⚡ Deals**, or search.
2. Add products to your cart – items from different shops go in **one cart**.
3. Check out and pay **cash on delivery** or by card.
4. Track your order in **My orders** and leave a review after delivery.

## Wholesale (B2B)
Businesses can open **🏭 Wholesale** for wholesale prices with a minimum quantity, or send a **quote request** to a seller. Answers come in **Quotes**.

## Selling on XpertCreation
Tap **Sell on XpertCreation**. You need a complete profile and an approved [ID check](../people/verify.md); our team approves each shop. Sellers manage products, stock with reorder levels, orders and their own buy and sell accounts.

Shop plans:
- **Bronze** – free, up to 10 live products
- **Gold** – up to 100 products and a Gold badge
- **Platinum** – up to 1,000 products, a Platinum badge and your shop featured on the Store home page

A **Product boost** shows a product first in the Store and in the showcase on the home page. See [Promote and paid items](promote.md).
