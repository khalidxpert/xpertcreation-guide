# Promote and paid items

Everything else on XpertCreation is free. These optional items help you get seen. Buy them at [xpertcreation.com/promote](https://xpertcreation.com/promote) (also **Prices & paid items** in the footer). Each is **one payment – nothing renews by itself**.

## What you can buy
- **Profile boost** – first in People search, with a Boosted mark. 7 days Rs 499 · 30 days Rs 1,499
- **Post boost** – your post at the top of For you, marked Sponsored, with live views and clicks. 3 days Rs 299 · 7 days Rs 599
- **Featured job** – pinned at the top of Jobs. 15 days Rs 999 · 30 days Rs 1,799
- **Company Pro** – PRO badge, all your open jobs featured, first in company search. 1 month Rs 2,499 · 1 year Rs 24,999
- **Blue tick** – identity check for the blue tick, Rs 999 once. **Fully refunded if the check is rejected.** See [The blue tick](../people/blue-tick.md).
- **Service boost** (Freelance) – 7 days Rs 499 · 30 days Rs 1,499
- **Property boost** – 7 days Rs 699 · 30 days Rs 1,999
- **Product boost** (Store) – 7 days Rs 599 · 30 days Rs 1,799
- **Store merchant plans** – Gold Rs 1,999 a month (100 products) · Platinum Rs 4,999 a month (1,000 products). Bronze stays free with 10.

Prices can change; the Promote page always shows the current price before you pay.

## How to pay
1. Open Promote, choose the item and what it is for (your profile, a post, a job…).
2. Pay with one of the methods below.
3. It starts as soon as the payment is confirmed. You get a receipt.

Ways to pay:
- **Card or wallet** through Safepay
- **JazzCash or Raast QR** – pay, then enter the transaction ID and your number; our team checks it
- **Google Play** inside the Xpert app

## My orders and receipts
[xpertcreation.com/orders](https://xpertcreation.com/orders) shows everything you bought: what is active and how many days are left, orders waiting for payment, and every payment with its method and a **Receipt** you can view or download.

Questions about a payment? See the **Refund policy** in the footer or contact [support](../help/support.md).
