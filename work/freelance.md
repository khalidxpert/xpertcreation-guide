# Freelance

At [xpertcreation.com/market](https://xpertcreation.com/market). Hire verified freelancers for design, writing, websites, Excel and more, or sell your own skills.

## Buying
- **Explore**: ready-made services (gigs) with packages and prices. Pick one and order.
- **Projects**: tap **+ Post a project**, describe the work and your budget, and choose from the bids you receive.
- Ask a seller for a **custom offer** if you need something different.

Payment is safe: **the seller is paid only when you accept the work.** Big jobs can be split into **milestones**. Files, messages and deadlines stay in the order, under **My orders**.

## Selling
1. Get your [ID verified](../people/verify.md) – only verified members can sell.
2. Open **Selling** and add your services with clear packages, prices and delivery times.
3. Deliver on time inside the order and ask for a review.

XpertCreation keeps **10%** of each sale. The rest goes to **Earnings**, which you can withdraw as cash.

## More
- **Saved**: services and projects you saved for later.
- **My CV**: build a CV from your profile and download it.
- **Team**: work together with other freelancers.
- A **Service boost** shows your service first, with a Featured badge. See [Promote and paid items](promote.md).
